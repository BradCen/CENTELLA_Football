# CENTELLA Football — First-impression UX

## Experience target

The opening should communicate **premium modern football**, not retro emulation and not a research simulator.

### 0–2 seconds — ignition
Black field, restrained sapphire stadium light, official CENTELLA symbol appears with a short light pulse. No fake glitch, pixel art, scanlines or arcade texture.

### 2–4 seconds — identity
CENTELLA wordmark resolves, `FOOTBALL` appears below with restrained letter spacing. Ambient pitch geometry is visible only as depth.

### Ready state
`PRESIONA CUALQUIER BOTÓN` pulses subtly. Keyboard, mouse or controller can continue. Input device is remembered later.

### Home
The UI uses a broadcast/product hybrid:

- large editorial hero on the left;
- six clear mode cards on the right;
- strong selected state instead of tiny cursors;
- persistent navigation hints;
- no fake feature availability: unfinished modes show their state;
- quick match is one confirmation away.

## Brand rules used

- Jet Black `#020203`
- Obsidian `#171717`
- Sapphire Blue `#2359AA`
- Pure White `#FFFFFF`
- Urbanist requested from the operating system; system sans-serif fallback if unavailable.
- Official wordmark/symbol geometry is preserved from the CENTELLA brand source.

## Inspiration boundary

The information density, controller-first navigation, cinematic transition discipline and clarity target the quality bar of modern FIFA/EA FC interfaces. Layout, motion, copy, colors, logo and assets are CENTELLA-original rather than copied screen-for-screen.
